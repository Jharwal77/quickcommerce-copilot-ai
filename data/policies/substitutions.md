# Substitutions Policy

This is a fictional policy document for Nimbus Now, an invented quick-commerce
operator. It exists to support retrieval evaluation and describes no real company.

## When a substitution is offered

A substitution is offered only when an ordered item is unavailable at the fulfilling dark
store after the order has been accepted. Substitution is never applied silently: the
customer receives a notification and has **90 seconds** to accept or reject before the
order is packed without the item and the line is refunded.

## Substitution rules

An acceptable substitute must satisfy all of the following:

- Same product and same brand, differing only in pack size, **or** the same product from a
  different brand within the same category.
- Price within **15 percent** of the original line price. If the substitute is more
  expensive, Nimbus Now absorbs the difference and the customer is never charged more.
  If it is cheaper, the difference is refunded.
- Matching dietary attributes. A vegetarian item is never substituted with a
  non-vegetarian item under any circumstance.
- Matching storage class. A chilled item is not substituted with an ambient item.

## Categories excluded from substitution

**Baby Care items are never substituted.** This includes diapers, baby wipes, and baby
lotion. The customer is refunded for the line instead. The exclusion exists because
caregiver brand and formulation preferences in Baby Care are not interchangeable.

Personal Care items are substituted only across pack sizes of the identical product, never
across brands.

## Out of stock without a substitute

If no acceptable substitute exists, the line is cancelled and refunded, and the rest of the
order proceeds. If the out-of-stock line is more than **60 percent** of the order value,
the customer is asked whether to cancel the whole order.

## Substitution rate targets

Operations targets a substitution rate below **6 percent** of order lines and an
out-of-stock rate below **4 percent** per dark store per day. Sustained breach of either
target triggers a replenishment review for that store.
