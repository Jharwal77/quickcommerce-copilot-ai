# Returns and Refunds Policy

This is a fictional policy document for Nimbus Now, an invented quick-commerce
operator. It exists to support retrieval evaluation and describes no real company.

## Return windows by category

Return eligibility depends on the product category, because perishability differs
sharply across a quick-commerce catalog.

- Fruits & Vegetables, Bakery, and Dairy & Eggs: report within **2 hours** of delivery.
- Frozen & Ready to Eat: report within **4 hours** of delivery, and the cold chain seal
  must be intact.
- Beverages, Snacks & Namkeen, Staples & Grains, Home & Cleaning: report within
  **48 hours** of delivery.
- Personal Care: report within **7 days** of delivery if the seal is unbroken.
- Baby Care: report within **7 days** of delivery. Opened diaper packs are not returnable
  unless the defect is a manufacturing fault.

## Non-returnable items

The following are never accepted for return, even inside the window above:

- Any item sold under a "Clearance" or "Near Expiry" tag.
- Cut fruit, cut vegetables, and any repacked fresh produce.
- Items where more than 30 percent of the pack has been consumed.

## Refund method and timelines

Refunds are issued to the original payment instrument by default.

- UPI and wallet: credited within **24 hours** of approval.
- Card: credited within **5 to 7 business days** of approval, subject to the issuing bank.
- Cash on delivery: credited to Nimbus Wallet within **24 hours**, or to a bank account
  within **3 business days** if the customer submits account details.

Refunds under **INR 200** are auto-approved without an agent review. Refunds of
**INR 200 or more** require a support agent to approve the claim.

## Damaged, missing, and spoiled items

For a damaged or spoiled perishable item, a photograph is required at the time of the
report. Missing items do not require a photograph. Once approved, the customer chooses a
refund or a replacement delivery; replacements ship from the same dark store that
fulfilled the original order and do not carry a fresh delivery fee.

## Repeat claim limits

A customer account may raise at most **4 approved perishable claims in a rolling 30-day
window**. Beyond that, further claims move to manual review. This limit exists to contain
refund abuse, which is the largest controllable loss line in perishable quick-commerce.
